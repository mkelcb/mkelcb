Privacy Policy - rclone Kubuntu

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Purpose

rclone Kubuntu is a personal application used solely by its owner to access the owner's Google Drive from a Kubuntu computer using rclone.

Google user data accessed

The application requests Google Drive access so that rclone can list, read, download, create, modify, move, and delete files and folders as instructed by the owner.

Use of Google user data

Google Drive data is used only to provide file access and synchronization functionality requested by the owner through rclone.

Storage

OAuth authorization information used by rclone is stored locally on the owner's computer. Files accessed through rclone may also be cached locally on that computer as part of normal filesystem operation.

Sharing and sale of data

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third parties by this application.

Website data collection

This informational website does not itself collect Google Drive data and does not provide access to the owner's Google Drive.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google Account access through their Google Account security settings at any time.
